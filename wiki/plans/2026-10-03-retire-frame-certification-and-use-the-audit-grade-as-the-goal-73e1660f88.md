# Retire frame certification and use the audit grade as the goal

## Summary

Designers will aim for **B or better and a ready result**, without a separate certification action. Keep the existing grade thresholds, axis weights, token-coverage cap, finding policies and waivers. Frame readiness still requires A/B, complete whole-file knowledge, no hard blockers and no unresolved scoring-critical reviews; report readiness still requires every audited frame to be ready.

Preserve existing certificate metadata, annotations, relaunch data, stored reports, cache formats and historical evidence. No external certificate consumer is known, and no migration is needed.

## Implementation

1. **Retire certification commands and presentation.** Remove `certify` and `certify-components` from message types, validation and controller dispatch; old raw requests must fail through the existing unsupported-message path. Remove `certified`, Certify controls, callbacks, eligibility calculations, progress state, certification-only locks and `certificationNotice`. Remove `CERTIFICATION_PAUSED` and `CERTIFICATION_PAUSED_MESSAGE`. Update active copy in Overview, App, Guidance, ProfileEditor, controller errors and historical-export guidance to describe grades, readiness and refresh. Preserve producer identity and the visible Development warning. Introduce no replacement approval step.

2. **Reject historical certification operations before document activity.** In `src/figma/mutations.ts`, immediately after `applyChangePlan` calls `assertContract`, synchronously scan the complete operation list and throw if any operation is `set-certification`, before entering per-operation preflight. In `src/plugin/main.ts`, scan every approved plan in `apply-all` immediately after plan lookup succeeds, before structural checkpoint creation or the apply loop. Rejection must precede clone validation, `saveVersionHistoryAsync`, `commitUndo`, transaction-marker writes and every earlier operation or plan in the request. Remove the certification branches from `applyOperation` and `verifyOperation`. Keep other cleanup behavior and rollback boundaries unchanged.

3. **Remove certification-only mechanics.** Delete `setCertification`, certification metadata capture/record/restore helpers and their snapshot type, `clearVariantCoverageAnnotations`, `VARIANT_COVERAGE_ANNOTATION_PREFIX`, `matchesCertificationTargetNames` and their unused imports/private helpers. Remove `recordCertificationAnnotations`, `MutationChangeGuard.expectAnnotations`, its signature map and exception branch. Remove the resulting empty `arm`/`clear` bookkeeping and unused `documentMutationIds` traversal. Retain the general document-change predicate: local plugin-data-only events remain exempt, while annotation, visual, remote and unknown changes invalidate knowledge. Preserve command serialization, build tokens, dirty-node tracking, structural recapture, resource verification, profile-draft locks, scan locks and Dev Mode restrictions for surviving actions. Keep generic annotation helpers and ordinary source-annotation cleanup.

4. **Remove the grading dependency while preserving compatibility.** Delete only `pipeline.certification-freshness` from fresh rule evaluation and its unused imports. Update the incomplete-knowledge message without changing its blocker behavior. In `src/figma/adapter.ts`, retain both certification-prefix filters in `hasAnnotations`: legacy stamps must not satisfy `pipeline.annotation`. Keep `NodeSnapshot.certification`, `parseCertificationSummary`, `CertificationSummary`, the raw certification input to `captureSupplement`, existing annotation signature inputs and cached fragment contents unchanged. These retained fields preserve capture/cache compatibility; no rule consumes the certificate summary after retirement.

5. **Keep historical contracts readable.** Retain `set-certification` in the change-plan JSON Schema and `ChangeOperation` union for historical data, together with its `riskForOperation` classification and exhaustive `operationOrder` entry. Fresh planning must not generate it. Keep `MultiFileReviewReportV1.certificationEligible:false`, its builder and v1 schema unchanged. Keep new readiness reports at schema 3 and retain support for historical report versions. Do not rewrite stored findings, grades, timestamps, hashes or provenance. Bump the plugin to `0.5.0` and ruleset to `1.0.0-beta.5`. Document that removing the formerly scored freshness rule can raise or lower fresh scores on previously certified targets without changing grade thresholds or rewriting historical results.

6. **Remove relaunch controls without cleaning node data.** Remove the sole `review-certification` relaunch entry and now-empty `relaunchButtons` property from both manifests. [Figma’s documented behavior](https://developers.figma.com/docs/plugins/api/properties/nodes-setrelaunchdata/) hides buttons whose commands are removed. Do not call `setRelaunchData` or delete certificate strings, source markers, grade notes or legacy variant notes. Preserve manifest identities, capabilities, permissions, document access and network settings.

7. **Retire obsolete QA instrumentation and update active guidance.** Remove certification entries from harness write/measured command sets, certification-only document-change observation and the UI’s `certified` run tracking. Old commands must be blocked even on allowlisted copies. Keep `qa-inspect` reads of exact `certification-v1` strings, full annotations and relaunch maps, plus ordinary audit timing, failure/cancellation evidence, byte provenance and write guards. Replace active certification timing/rollback instructions with retirement verification guidance. Update README/package copy and relevant active wiki guidance; preserve prior raw exports and immutable archived plans.
