# Existing-frame destination and appearance handoff

[Issue 96](https://github.com/JFusco/design-passport/issues/96) adds an explicit independent-frame path alongside the existing workspace submission. The plugin reads cascading accessible destinations, names the full Design-tab destination on confirmation and captures an editable design/variant name, appearance and configured breakpoint. It rejects changed selections, mismatched widths and known default conflicts before confirmation. Unknown save results retain their original identity for safe retry after a restart.

The matching Conductor endpoint scopes reads to the token's organization and project assignments/admin policy. Its transaction rechecks the destination hierarchy and exact configured width, preserves the existing default, rejects duplicate frame/name conflicts and saves the design, discussion and annotation receipt together. Independent receipts do not fabricate a workspace connection. Approval, workflow transitions and automatic change/Slack reporting are outside this handoff.
