# Apply Cumulative styles to the companion

[Issue 148](https://github.com/JFusco/design-passport/issues/148) brings the
Cumulative palette, type, spacing, and motion to the companion. Page titles
name their tasks. The loading mark appears with status text during navigation
and requests. Shared spacing tokens align the layout and reserve room for
dropdown chevrons. The Approved guidance section names projects and explains
what each file does in Figma. Its buttons preserve contrast on hover and focus.

The companion keeps content readable during keyboard navigation and honors
reduced motion. Aeonik and Aeonik Fono load from locally supplied font files.
See [companion styles](../topics/plugin-style-parity.md#companion-styles) for
font setup and the design decisions.
